How it works

Four steps, no screen required

The app uses Android's notification system, the same mechanism you already use when you reply from the lock screen.

  1. 1

    Detect

    A notification listener picks up the incoming Instagram DM on your device.

  2. 2

    Match

    Your rules decide who gets answered, using keyword, regex, or contact filters.

  3. 3

    Generate

    The chosen AI provider writes a reply in the tone you configured.

  4. 4

    Send

    The reply is delivered through Instagram's own notification Reply action.

Inside the app

What you control

Every part of the reply flow is configurable from the dashboard. These are real screens from the app.

Your inbox, at a glance

The dashboard shows reply activity, active rules, and live status so you always know the bot is working.

  • Daily reply counts and recent activity
  • Toggles for notification and accessibility access
  • Last-attempt status for quick debugging

Rules that fit your inbox

Decide who gets an answer and how. Match on exact text, a pattern, or a sender list, and cap replies per day.

  • Trigger types: contains, exact, starts/ends with, regex, any
  • Per-rule AI provider and optional fixed template
  • Human-like delay and daily reply limits

Pick the AI you trust

Connect a free provider or a paid one. Keys are stored locally on the device and sent only to the provider you choose.

  • Free: Groq, OpenCode Zen, Gemini (no card)
  • Paid: OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, OpenAI, custom
  • Built-in model lists and key validation

One reply, every time

Duplicate notifications can't produce duplicate replies. The engine deduplicates by sender and message, and remembers what it already sent.

  • 60-second in-memory dedupe
  • Persisted reply log that survives restarts
  • Single send per message, by design
